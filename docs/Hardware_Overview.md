**Arduino Nano ESP32-S3**

16 MB flash storage, 8 MB external PSRAM, and 512 KB internal SRAM, plus 16 KB RTC SRAM usable by the ULP low power coprocessor. The CPU is a dual-core Xtensa LX7 with a maximum clock of 240 MHz. The external IO bus runs at 80 MHz.
